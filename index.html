Constitution Highlights (APS)

Identity & Core Values
Name: Bangladesh Youth Community Verona (APS). Core values: Nationalism, Secularism, Socialism, and Democracy.
The 7 Cooperative Principles
1. Open Membership 2. Democratic Control 3. Economic Participation 4. Autonomy 5. Education 6. Cooperation 7. Community Interest.
Membership Rights & Fees
The annual fee grants the right to vote, elect organs, view social books, and call assemblies (with 10% backing).
Declaration of Commitment
I promise to respect the rules, maintain unity, and collaborate for the common good without causing divisions.

Humanitarian Aid

On behalf of the Bangladesh Youth Community Verona, Italy, we have played an active role in humanitarian assistance in recent times. Notable initiatives include the formation of an emergency fund to support the flood-affected people of Noakhali in August 2024, providing financial assistance to repatriate deceased individuals to Bangladesh, and offering financial support for medical treatment.

"Bangladesh Youth Community Verona, Italy, remains always committed to the welfare of humanity."
